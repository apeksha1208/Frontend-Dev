frontend: The part of a website or app that the user sees and interacts with.
Eg:When you open YouTube, the buttons, videos list, search bar, and like/dislike icons all are part of the frontend.

backend:The part that works behind the scenes .it handles data, logic, and communication with the database.
eg:When you log in to YouTube, the backend checks if your username and password are correct by talking to the database, then loads your personalized videos.

Full Stack:A full-stack developer can work on both frontend and backend.
Eg:A full-stack developer can create a blog website where users:
See and write posts (frontend)

Store those posts in a database (backend)
