HTML Quotations

Long Quotation (Blockquote)

Here is a paragraph that contains a long quotation:

"The only way to do great work is to love what you do. If you haven't found it yet, keep looking. Don't settle. As with all matters of the heart, you'll know when you find it. And, like any great relationship, it just gets better and better as the years roll on."

— Steve Jobs

Short Quotation (Inline Quote)

This paragraph contains a short quotation: Knowledge is power - a famous saying that emphasizes the importance of learning.

Quotation with HTML Entities

Here's a quote that includes HTML entities:

"In programming, we often use symbols like < and > for comparisons, and & for logical operations. These are essential in HTML & CSS development."

This demonstrates how HTML entities like &lt;, &gt;, and &amp; are used within quoted text.

Mixed Quotations

Sometimes we need both types: Short quotes work well within paragraphs, while longer quotes like the one below need more space:

"The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams."

— Eleanor Roosevelt

Note: The <blockquote> element is used for longer quotations that should be visually separated, while <q> is used for short, inline quotations.
