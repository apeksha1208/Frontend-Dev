HTML Entities Examples

This paragraph demonstrates the use of HTML entities to display special characters:

  • Less than symbol: <
  • Greater than symbol: >
  • Ampersand symbol: &
  • Double quote symbol: "
  • Single quote symbol: '

This paragraph shows trademark and copyright symbols using HTML entities:

  • Copyright symbol: © 2024
  • Trademark symbol: Trademark™
  • Registered trademark symbol: Registered®

Note: HTML entities are used to display characters that have special meaning in HTML or characters that are not easily typed on a keyboard.

Example of entities in a quote: "The quick brown fox & the lazy dog <3"
